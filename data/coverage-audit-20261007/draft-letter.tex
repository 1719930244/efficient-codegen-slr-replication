    \item \textbf{The 2{,}110 candidates.} We agree that this step limits coverage, and we now quantify it. The lower-ranked candidates can no longer be screened individually, so we estimated the cost of the cut with an independent audit. We re-ran the query on OpenAlex for the original search window, drew a random sample of 400 records outside the corpus, screened them with two automated screeners from different model families, and adjudicated every record that either screener did not exclude. About 1.25\% of the sampled records are eligible at the abstract level, which implies roughly 47 eligible studies missing from the literature that OpenAlex indexes, with a 95\% interval of 20 to 110, and a recall of about 0.68 for that literature. Section~3.3 and Threats to Validity report the audit and its limits, and the eligible records it found are being confirmed by the authors.
