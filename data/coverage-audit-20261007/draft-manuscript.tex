\re{To bound what the retrieval cut may have cost, we ran an independent coverage audit. The three-group query was re-executed on OpenAlex over titles and abstracts for 1 January 2017 to 6 March 2026 and returned 3{,}891 records, 99 of which are primary studies of this review. Two automated screeners from different model families screened a simple random sample of 400 of the remaining 3{,}792 records, agreeing on 88\% of them with a Cohen's kappa of 0.71, and every record that either screener did not exclude was adjudicated on its abstract. Five sampled records meet the criteria at the abstract level, which implies about 47 eligible studies, with a 95\% interval of 20 to 110, that OpenAlex indexes but the corpus lacks, and a recall of about 0.68 for the literature visible to OpenAlex. A further 30 records are generic efficiency methods that list code generation among their evaluation tasks, so the estimate depends on how strictly IC3 is read, and the eligible records still await author confirmation.}
